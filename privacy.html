Lyrichord Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Lyrichord is a songwriting notebook: you write lyrics, put chords over them, play the chords and record your voice. This policy explains what happens to your data. In short: your songs and recordings stay on your phone. The app sends only anonymous crash reports and feature-usage counts, and you can turn that off.

What stays on your device

  • Songs (lyrics, chords, titles) are stored as files on your phone.
  • Voice recordings you make over the chords and voice notes (Ideas) are stored on your phone.
  • Settings (language, headphone latency, stage mode text size) are stored on your phone.

We do not have servers that receive your songs or recordings, and we cannot see them.

Microphone

The app asks for microphone access only for these features, and uses the microphone only while you use them: - recording your voice over the chords, and voice notes (Ideas), saved on your phone; - the tuner, which listens to find the note of your string and saves nothing; - dictating chords by voice, if you use it.

Voice chord entry

If you tap the microphone to say chords, your phone's speech recognition service turns your speech into text. On most Android phones this is Google's speech service, which may process the audio on Google's servers under Google's Privacy Policy. The app receives only the recognised text and keeps only the chords. You can always type chords instead.

Backups and sharing

  • If Android backup is turned on in your phone settings, your songs are included in your phone's backup to your Google account, under Google's terms. You can turn this off in your phone settings.
  • When you choose Back up all songs, Share as PDF or Copy for Suno, the file or text goes only where you send it (for example Google Drive, email or another app).

Purchases

Subscriptions are paid through Google Play. We do not receive your card details, name or email.

To check whether Pro is active, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription service. RevenueCat receives a random app user ID created by the app, your purchase details from Google Play (which plan, purchase and renewal dates, price and currency, store country) and basic device information (app version, Android version). It does not receive your songs, recordings or Google account. RevenueCat processes this data on our behalf under its privacy policy. Purchase records are kept while your subscription is active and as long as needed for accounting and tax obligations.

Crash reports and usage statistics

To fix crashes and see which features help people, the app uses Firebase Crashlytics and Google Analytics for Firebase, services provided by Google. - Crash reports contain technical details of the error (where in the app it happened), the phone model, Android version and app version. - Usage statistics record app events such as "song created", "chords played", "stage mode opened", "Pro screen shown" or "subscription started", with a random app-instance identifier, approximate country (from the network) and device information. - They never include your lyrics, song titles, chords, recordings, contacts or account details. - The advertising ID is not collected, ad personalisation is off, and the data is not used for ads or sold. - Data is processed by Google under the Firebase privacy information and kept for a limited time (crash reports up to 90 days, usage statistics up to 14 months).

You can turn both off at any time in Settings → Help improve Lyrichord.

Ads

The app shows no ads and does not sell personal data or share it for advertising. The services named above (Google Firebase, RevenueCat) process data only on our behalf.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Deleting your data

Delete a song in the app, or uninstall the app to remove all songs, recordings and settings from your phone. Copies you exported or backed up are under your control.

To have crash reports, usage statistics or purchase records deleted from Firebase and RevenueCat, email us from the address in the Contact section. Records we must keep by law (for example for tax) are kept only as long as required.

Contact

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
