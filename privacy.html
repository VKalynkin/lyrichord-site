Lyrichord Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Lyrichord is a songwriting notebook: you write lyrics, put chords over them, play the chords and record your voice. This policy explains what happens to your data. In short: your songs and recordings stay on your phone, and we do not collect personal data.

What stays on your device

  • Songs (lyrics, chords, titles) are stored as files on your phone.
  • Voice recordings you make over the chords are stored on your phone.
  • Settings (language, headphone latency, stage mode text size) are stored on your phone.

We do not have servers that receive your songs or recordings, and we cannot see them.

Microphone

The app asks for microphone access only to record your voice over the chords and, if you use it, to dictate chords by voice. Recording happens only while you press Record or the microphone button.

Voice chord entry

If you tap the microphone to say chords, your phone's speech recognition service turns your speech into text. On most Android phones this is Google's speech service, which may process the audio on Google's servers under Google's Privacy Policy. The app receives only the recognised text and keeps only the chords. You can always type chords instead.

Backups and sharing

  • If Android backup is turned on in your phone settings, your songs are included in your phone's backup to your Google account, under Google's terms. You can turn this off in your phone settings.
  • When you choose Back up all songs, Share as PDF or Copy for Suno, the file or text goes only where you send it (for example Google Drive, email or another app).

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Google Play. We do not receive your card details. We only learn whether your subscription is active, to unlock Pro features.

Analytics and ads

The app shows no ads and does not sell or share personal data. If we add anonymous usage statistics in the future (for example, how many people finish the welcome screens), we will update this policy first and never include your songs, recordings or contact details.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Deleting your data

Delete a song in the app, or uninstall the app to remove all songs, recordings and settings from your phone. Copies you exported or backed up are under your control.

Contact

Questions about privacy: lyrichord.songs@gmail.com

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
